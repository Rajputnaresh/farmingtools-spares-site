### Overall Impression

An operationally robust, high-integrity dashboard tailored for Indian agricultural spare parts distribution. It bridges executive analytics with frontline dealer dispatching while maintaining strict touch ergonomics and outdoor contrast standards.

---

### What's Working

1. **Grounded Physical Operations**: Deep domain realism with 1,746 catalog SKUs, mechanical fitment specs, transport Bilty numbers, and drop-dispatch hubs.
2. **Hardened Outdoor Visibility**: High-contrast text pairing (15.8:1 AAA), terracotta units curve (6.5:1), and deep forest ink on WhatsApp green (9.1:1 AAA).
3. **Resilient Local Persistence**: Logged dealer requests survive page refreshes and network interruptions via `localStorage`.

### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Read-Only Dispatches Prevent Order Lifecycle Progression**
  - **Why it matters**: Consignments transition from "Challan Issued" (Pending) to "Dispatched" (Completed) once the transporter confirms the Bilty. Currently, table rows and mobile cards cannot be updated inline.
  - **Fix**: Add an interactive status toggle directly on the status badge in `DrillDownTable.tsx` to switch Pending ↔ Completed and persist back to `localStorage`.
  - **Suggested Command**: `/impeccable harden dashboard`

- **[P2] Flat Dealer List Violates Cognitive Chunking in Modal**
  - **Why it matters**: `NewRequestModal.tsx` lists 10 flat dealer options without categorization, violating Miller's Law ($\le 4$ items per group).
  - **Fix**: Group dealers using `<optgroup>` by regional hub (`Rajasthan Hub`, `Punjab Hub`, `Other Hubs`).
  - **Suggested Command**: `/impeccable clarify dashboard`

- **[P2] Mobile Chart Height Pushes Actionable Feed Below the Fold**
  - **Why it matters**: On 390px mobile screens, the 288px AreaChart pushes the actionable dispatch card feed nearly two viewports down.
  - **Fix**: On mobile (`sm:hidden`), collapse the chart or provide an expandable toggle so the dispatch card feed appears immediately below the KPI summary.
  - **Suggested Command**: `/impeccable adapt dashboard`

- **[P3] Eliminate `side-tab` (`border-l-4`) Warning in `DrillDownTable.tsx`**
  - **Why it matters**: Flags detector heuristic warnings.
  - **Fix**: Replace `border-l-4` with an emerald ring and subtle background wash.
  - **Suggested Command**: `/impeccable polish dashboard`
