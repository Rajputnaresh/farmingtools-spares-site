## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM Assessment**: While the underlying data layer is richly populated with authentic KrishiGears catalog SKUs (`SP-001`, `ATC-001`, `P58-008`) and real regional dealer destinations (`Jaipur Krishi Kendra`, `Sharma Agro Spares - Kota`), the interface structure and visual styling are an off-the-shelf B2B SaaS analytics clone. The header and footer literally leak developer architectural slogans into user-facing chrome (*"Overview • Zoom & Filter • Details-on-Demand"*, *"Progressive Disclosure Architecture"*). The palette relies on default Tailwind Slate ramps and tech-indigo rather than the warm agricultural design tokens mandated in `DESIGN.md`. Furthermore, it ignores KrishiGears' core structural reality: as a zero-inventory drop-dispatch business, the crucial signals are OEM supplier lead times, dispatch aging, fitment validation, and bilti tracking—not generic SaaS "demand velocity".

**Deterministic & Browser Scan**:
- **CLI Detector**: Reported 2 warnings (`gray-on-color` in `EmptyState.tsx:45`). Both were confirmed **false positives** caused by regex matching ignoring Tailwind state prefixes (`hover:text-emerald-800` on `hover:bg-emerald-50`).
- **In-Browser Detector (Computed Styles & Geometry)**: Injected `/detect.js` via headless Chromium and uncovered **15 genuine UI anti-patterns**:
  - **6x `low-contrast` (2.5:1–2.6:1 vs 4.5:1 required)**: `text-slate-400` (`#94a3b8`) on white cards and `#f8fafc` across section headings, KPI subtitles, captions, and filter labels.
  - **5x `undersized-ui-text` (10px)**: Subsystem badges in the table rows (`text-[10px]`) drop below the 11px accessible UI floor.
  - **`line-length` (113 chars)**: Instructions in `EmptyState.tsx` exceed readable bounds.
  - **`icon-tile-stack`**: 48×48px icon tile stacked directly above heading `h3` in `EmptyState.tsx`.
  - **2x `nested-cards`**: Card-inside-card DOM structures in the drill-down section.
  - **`overused-font`**: Fallback to Roboto/system-sans rather than brand Mukta.

## Overall Impression
The dashboard has genuine agricultural spares data and a sound three-tier progressive disclosure flow, but its presentation is trapped in generic SaaS fintech tropes: low-contrast cool slate greys that wash out completely under bright sunlight, sub-44px touch targets that frustrate mobile thumbs, and developer slogans that alienate rural counter dealers. Grounding it in KrishiGears' warm agricultural design tokens and adding 1-tap WhatsApp dispatch triage will turn this from a tech demo into an indispensable daily operational tool.

## What's Working
1. **Authentic Domain Grounding**: Uses genuine KrishiGears catalog SKUs (`GEAR CASE 28MM 9T`, `ATC-001`, `P58-008`), real INR currency with Indian numeral formatting (`₹1,96,000`), and genuine regional dealer hubs (`Jaipur`, `Kota`, `Alwar`, `Bathinda`).
2. **Three-Tier Progressive Disclosure Architecture**: The Macro-Overview $\rightarrow$ Trend Exploration $\rightarrow$ Granular Line-Item drilldown cleanly prevents mobile DOM bloat and keeps page weight lightweight on metered connections.
3. **Bilingual Regional Terminology**: Devanagari operational terminology on badges (`भेजा गया`, `बिलिंग`, `मंगाया जा सकता है`, `कुल पुर्जे`) provides immediate familiarity for Hindi-speaking dealers.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Severe Sunlight Contrast Failures & Design System Violation (The Cool Slate Glare)
- **Why it matters**: `text-slate-400` yields a 2.5:1 contrast ratio against white cards and `#f8fafc`, failing WCAG AA (4.5:1). In 40,000+ lux daylight on a budget Android phone in Rajasthan, headings, subtitles, and axes wash out into an invisible grey haze. Furthermore, cool slate violates `DESIGN.md`'s strict **"No Cool Grey Rule"**.
- **Fix**: Purge all `slate-*` classes. Replace with warm agricultural tokens from `DESIGN.md`: ink (`#1c2420`), body-ink (`#3d4a42`), muted-ink (`#5f6f66`), hairline (`#dbe7de`), and page ground (`#f6f8f5`). Replace tech-indigo (`#6366f1`) with standing crop green (`#1b7a43`) and harvest amber (`#f0b429`). Ensure all text exceeds 4.5:1 (WCAG AAA target 7:1 for sunlight).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

### [P1] Sub-44px Mobile Touch Targets & Fumbled Touch Controls
- **Why it matters**: Month selection pills (26px), category filter selects (32px), theme toggle (36px), and clear buttons (18px) violate the 44×44px minimum touch target standard, causing frequent mis-taps outdoors on dusty mobile screens.
- **Fix**: Enforce strict `min-h-[44px]` and `min-w-[44px]` on all interactive elements. Turn the chart month filter into a generous segmented touch control (`h-11 px-4 text-sm font-semibold`), and pad the drill-down clear button hit target to 44px.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Deceptive State Syncing Between KPI Cards & Drill-Down Selection
- **Why it matters**: When a user drills down into January (₹1,96,000), the KPI summary cards above stay frozen on aggregate Q1 turnover (₹6,96,200). Users assume the summary cards reflect their active filter, leading to confusion over actual monthly sales.
- **Fix**: Synchronize KPI summary cards with the active drill-down selection (displaying January's turnover, volume, and dispatch rate when Jan is active), and add an explicit scope indicator: `Showing: Jan 2026 [Reset to All]`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

### [P2] Elimination of Academic Developer Jargon from End-User Chrome
- **Why it matters**: Printing software design slogans like *"Progressive Disclosure Architecture"*, *"Overview • Zoom & Filter • Details-on-Demand"*, and *"Demand Velocity"* in headers and footers feels amateur and alienates rural shopkeepers.
- **Fix**: Replace developer slogans with clear bilingual trade terminology: *"मासिक ऑर्डर व पुर्जे (Monthly Dispatches & Volume)"*, *"चालान व बिलिंग विवरण (Order Challans & Part Details)"*. Load and apply the **Mukta** typeface across Latin and Devanagari strings.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

### [P2] Lack of Dispatch Triage & 1-Tap WhatsApp Dealer Export
- **Why it matters**: Alex and counter shopkeepers cannot search by part SKU (`P58-008`), cannot filter the table to see only stalled/`Pending` orders, and cannot export or share dispatch confirmations over WhatsApp (where 90% of rural Indian agricultural machinery trade occurs).
- **Fix**: Add an instant SKU/dealer search input above the table, add a quick status pill toggle (`All` | `Dispatched` | `Challan Issued`), and add a 1-tap WhatsApp share button on each dispatch row formatting an instant Hindi dispatch message.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable shape`

## Persona Red Flags

- Cannot search for a specific SKU (`SP-001`) or dealer name (`Jaipur Krishi Kendra`) when on a support call.

- Subsystem categorization badges use microscopic 10px type (`text-[10px]`), falling below the 11px accessibility floor.
- Turnover and Volume are differentiated in the chart solely by line color with identical stroke styles and curve shapes, failing users with color blindness.

**Ramesh (Rural Counter Shopkeeper / Farmer outdoors in direct sunlight on low-end Android)**:
- Faint Slate-200 borders and 0.45-opacity chart fills wash out into a uniform glare under 40,000+ lux midday Rajasthan sun. If dark mode is toggled, the screen becomes a black mirror.
- Thumb taps on 26px month buttons and 18px close icons trigger frequent mis-clicks.
- English developer slogans (*"Progressive Disclosure Architecture"*, *"Demand Velocity"*) create confusion; requires straightforward Hindi/Hinglish phrasing (*"चालान सूची"*, *"पुर्जों की मांग"*).
