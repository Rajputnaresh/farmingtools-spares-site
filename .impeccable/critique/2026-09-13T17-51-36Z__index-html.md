#### Overall Impression

The rare operations app whose real constraints - sunlight, one hand, Hindi/English, no online payment - are visibly baked into the design instead of bolted on. It scores like a much larger product's UI: 9.36:1 primary-button contrast, 42/42 labeled buttons, an active service worker. #### What's Working

1. **Outdoor-first daylight discipline.** color-scheme light-only, #0F5132 system with 9.36:1 primary-button contrast and 17.06:1 body text - deliberate and correct for direct-sun counters on cheap Android screens.
2. **Counter Sale Mode.** Markup multiplier (1x-20x), steppers, built-in counter bill with WhatsApp send - the counter clerk's whole workflow in one surface.
3. **A floor higher than most shipping apps.** 42/42 buttons accessible-named, :focus-visible in 5 selector groups, a skip link, tablist/tab roles, decorative alt="" used correctly, service worker registered and active, catalog preloaded (~550ms saved).

#### Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Registration: 8+ fields, no progressive disclosure.** A new dealer on a phone faces one long form (business, phone, email, address, city, PIN, GSTIN, UPI, password). Why it matters: this is the app's growth door; abandonment here costs every downstream dealer action. Fix: chunk into 3-4 steps (identity -> location -> trade credentials -> password) with per-step inline validation. Suggested command: `$impeccable onboard`.
2. **[P1] Help and documentation gap (H10=2).** The app assumes dealer familiarity; "Requests", "Dispatch", and the markup concept arrive with no explanation, and the "importer will approve it" message sets an expectation with no visible approval status. Fix: first-use contextual hints, a short first-run FAQ, inline definitions for trade terms. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.
3. **[P2] PO submission without a confirmation step.** Cart -> Generate PO is one tap; combined with a dismissible Counter Bill modal (staged items lost on dismiss), mistakes are cheap to make and hard to see. Fix: a confirm sheet (items, qty, value) before PO generation; persist staged counter-bill items. Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`.
4. **[P2] aria-expanded not synced on the Parts-Finder toggle (#pf-toggle-bar).** Screen readers will not announce expansion state even though the visual chevron updates. Fix: set aria-expanded/aria-controls in the same handler that swaps the chevron. Suggested command: `$impeccable audit`.
5. **[P2] Item-card action density and God-Mode divergence.** Cards pack 3-4 competing actions into compact rows; the admin surface reads like a different product. Fix: collapse secondary card actions into a per-card menu; bring admin views onto the same token system. Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`.
