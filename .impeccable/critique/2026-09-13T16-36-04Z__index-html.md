#### Overall Impression
KrishiGears Spares is an exceptionally resilient, domain-aware operational PWA with authentic trade workflows and strong daylight legibility. The biggest opportunity is reducing card action density for field farmers while introducing the promised Marathi language layer.

#### What's Working
1. **Sunlight-Engineered Display Architecture**: Strict light-only enforcement, `#0F5132` high-contrast green, and generous 44–48px touch targets ensure outdoor readability on budget LCD phones.
2. **Defensive Commercial Guardrails**: Unpriced parts cannot check out at ₹0, blocking catastrophic margin disputes; offline orders queue reliably in IndexedDB with idempotency keys.
3. **Counter Sale Billing Bridge**: Shopkeepers can bill walk-in farmers at custom retail markups and dispatch formatted WhatsApp PDF invoices in seconds.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Mobile Item Card Action Clutter & Touch Interference**
  - *Why it matters*: Catalog list items pack up to 3 action targets (`Catalog Diagram`, `+ Bill`, `+ Add`) alongside the thumbnail, risking mis-taps with calloused thumbs in harsh sun.
  - *Fix*: Hide `+ Bill` by default on retail view (show only when Counter Sale Mode is enabled). Simplify the diagram button into a subtle secondary icon affordance.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable layout`

- **[P1] Complete Absence of Promised Marathi Localization**
  - *Why it matters*: The project brief specifically targets Maharashtra farm machinery belts, yet zero Marathi translations exist (only English and Hindi are implemented).
  - *Fix*: Add Marathi (`mr`) language bundle and update `setLanguage` to support tri-lingual switching.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable clarify`

- **[P2] Parts Finder Subsystem Jargon Barrier**
  - *Why it matters*: Subsystem chips ("Crankcase", "Carburettor", "Transmission Case") rely on Latin English terms. Farmers search by colloquial vernacular symptoms ("तेल सील", "क्लच कटोरी").
  - *Fix*: Add bilingual Devanagari labels and visual assembly icons to subsystem chips.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable clarify`

- **[P3] Administrative UI Drift in God Mode**
  - *Why it matters*: God Mode tenant management uses raw, unstyled tables and compressed KPI bars that deviate from the core design tokens.
  - *Fix*: Harmonize administrative cards and tables with `--r-lg`, `--shadow-sm`, and proper typography tokens.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable polish`

#### Persona Red Flags

- **Farmer Ramesh (Outdoor Android User in Sunlight)**: The HTML `<select>` elements in the Parts Finder trigger Android's native system picker dialog with unstyled low-contrast text that washes out in bright glare.
- **Dealer Suresh (Busy Shop Counter in Jaipur)**: Cannot scan barcodes or perform rapid multi-line entry at the counter; unpriced parts cannot have quick manual price overrides during bill creation.
- **Distributor Amit (Managing Shipments in Indore)**: The Dispatch screen lacks batch filtering by transporter or destination district, forcing extensive scrolling across long order lists.

#### Minor Observations
- The header brand button (`#home-brand`) triggers a full view reset on accidental tap during normal thumb scrolling.
- Toast notices sit at `bottom: 76px`, occasionally covering bottom navigation tabs on small screens.
- Proforma PDF export converts ₹ to `Rs.` and strips non-ASCII Devanagari characters due to standard Latin font limits.
